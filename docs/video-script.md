# RonVoice — video script

Roughly 2 minutes 40 seconds spoken at a normal pace.
Short sentences on purpose: easier to read out loud, easier to cut.

Numbers in this script are checked against the build: 32 working orders,
158 English phrases across them.

---

## 1 — Cold open (0:00 – 0:15)

**ON SCREEN:** gameplay. You are facing a door. Say the line, squad stacks up.

> "Stack up."
>
> "Open with flashbang."

*(let the squad act, no talking over it)*

**YOU SAY:**

> I didn't touch the command menu once.

---

## 2 — What it is (0:15 – 0:40)

**ON SCREEN:** the app window, Commands tab.

**YOU SAY:**

> This is RonVoice. It lets you command your squad in Ready or Not by
> talking, instead of holding the menu and picking through it.
>
> It's free, and it runs entirely on your own machine. Nothing is sent to
> the internet, and it works with no connection at all.
>
> And it only listens while the game is in focus. Alt-tab to Discord and
> it stops on its own.

---

## 3 — The catalogue (0:40 – 1:05)

**ON SCREEN:** scroll the Commands tab. Type in the search box: `flash`.

**YOU SAY:**

> Every order it knows is listed here, so you never have to guess what you
> can say.
>
> Right now there are thirty-two orders, and about a hundred and fifty
> different ways to say them. You don't have to remember the exact words —
> "open with flashbang", "flash and clear", "bang it" all reach the same
> order.
>
> Search finds them however you type. Words out of order, half a word,
> doesn't matter.

---

## 4 — It tells you when something is wrong (1:05 – 1:25)

**ON SCREEN:** point at the status strip at the top.

**YOU SAY:**

> This strip is the part I care most about.
>
> Every way this kind of tool fails is silent. Wrong microphone, no
> permissions, game not in focus — you speak, nothing happens, and there is
> no error anywhere.
>
> So it says all of that out loud, all the time. If something is wrong,
> it's the first thing on the screen.

---

## 5 — Teach it your own words (1:25 – 1:50)

**ON SCREEN:** click "+ minha frase" on an order, type a phrase, press Enter.
Then try one that collides and let the refusal show.

**YOU SAY:**

> If you say something it doesn't know, you teach it. Pick the order, type
> how you actually say it, and it's yours.
>
> And if your phrase already belongs to a different order, it refuses and
> tells you which one. That's on purpose. Two orders sharing a phrase would
> break both of them, and you'd never find out why.
>
> You can also export your phrases and send them to a friend.

---

## 6 — VR (1:50 – 2:15)

**ON SCREEN:** headset view, VR gameplay, give an order by voice.

**YOU SAY:**

> It also works in VR. This is the VRO mod, and voice control fits it
> better than anything — you have no keyboard, and both hands are busy.
>
> Getting there took some digging. In VR the command menu opens, but it
> won't take the selection. So RonVoice doesn't use the menu at all. It
> talks to the game through a mod, which means one key press per order,
> and no menu to fight with.

---

## 7 — What you need, and what's missing (2:15 – 2:35)

**ON SCREEN:** the readme, or the settings tab.

**YOU SAY:**

> You'll need two things. Ready or Not on Windows, and the RoNSpeech mod
> installed — that's what actually gives the orders.
>
> Two honest notes. It has thirty-two orders today, not everything in the
> game, and the app marks exactly which ones are missing so you're never
> guessing. And English is the language that works well right now.
> Portuguese is in there, but it still gets things wrong.

---

## 8 — Close (2:35 – 2:45)

**ON SCREEN:** the GitHub page.

**YOU SAY:**

> It's free, and the code is open. Link is below.
>
> If you try it and something doesn't work, tell me which command — that
> helps more than anything else.

---

## Notes before recording

- Record the game audio, and speak the orders clearly and a little slower
  than normal. The recogniser is good, but a video is not the place to
  find its limits.
- Show a failure on purpose if you can — the refusal message when a phrase
  collides. It reads as confidence, not as a bug.
- Do NOT promise all seventy orders. The app shows 32 working and 38
  pending, and someone will count.
- The link needs a Release with the zip before this goes out, or people
  arrive at source code they can't run.
